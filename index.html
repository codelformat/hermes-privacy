Hermes Agent — personal instance

This is a private, self-hosted AI assistant run by a single individual (GitHub: codelformat) for their own use. It is not a public service and does not accept other users.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22

Data accessed

With the owner's explicit OAuth consent, the app accesses the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar data to read, organize and draft email and manage calendar events on the owner's behalf.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is processed on a computer controlled by the owner and is used solely to provide assistant features to the owner.
  • Content may be sent to the owner's chosen AI model provider to generate responses; it is not used for advertising.
  • Data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any other third party.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Terms of Service

This app is a personal tool used only by its owner. No service is offered to anyone else, and it is provided as-is without warranty.
